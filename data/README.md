## Dataset Information

**Name:** PEMS-BAY (Bay Area Traffic Speed)  
**Source:** California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Performance Measurement System (PeMS)  
**Coverage:** January 1 – June 30, 2017  
**Sensors:** 325 loop detectors in the San Francisco Bay Area  
**Sampling Interval:** 5 minutes  
**Measurement:** Traffic speed in **miles per hour (mph)**  
**Original Format:** Wide — one column per sensor, rows are timestamps  
**Cleaned Format:** Long — columns: `timestamp`, `sensor_id`, `observed_speed`, `outlier_flag`

## Limitations

- The CSV does not include **GPS coordinates** for sensors. The geospatial map is disabled unless you supply `sensor_locations.csv`.
- The CSV does not include **vehicle counts, occupancy, weather, or incident data**. These fields are nullable in the database schema.
- Speed limits for individual roads are not embedded in the dataset. Congestion thresholds are demonstration rules.
- This dataset covers only 6 months (Jan–Jun 2017) and may not generalise to other seasons or years.

## Optional: Enabling the Map

To enable the Geospatial Analytics page:

1. Obtain verified sensor coordinates from the [Caltrans PeMS portal](https://pems.dot.ca.gov/).
2. Create `data/sensor_locations.csv` with these exact columns:

```csv
sensor_id,latitude,longitude,area_name,road_type
400001,37.3382,-121.8863,San Jose,Freeway
400017,37.3590,-121.9252,San Jose,Freeway
...
```

3. Restart the Streamlit app.
